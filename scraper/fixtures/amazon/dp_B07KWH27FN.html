    This is some of the tastiest peanut butter I've ever had, with an excellent texture. I bought it mostly to use in sauces, mainly for wuhan noodles, but I always ended up eating a few spoonfuls straight from the jar when I use it. It worked great in the sauce for the noodles because of how readily it mixed with the other ingredients and how it spread out to evenly coat the noodles without difficulty. It was convenient not having to stir it when I opened it, and there was minimal separation as I used it up. Wasn't too greasy, was just sweet enough without being like a reeses. pleasantly nutty with a warm earthiness, no bitter dirt like flavor, no acrid off notes, not overtly salty, just enough to bring out the warmth of the flavor. I would buy this again and again if it were available.
